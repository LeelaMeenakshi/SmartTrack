User Guide

Understand how Smart Track works before using

1. Introduction

SmartTrack is a web application developed to help B.Tech students of IIT Gandhinagar manage their academic journey. It allows students to record completed courses, monitor graduation requirements, analyze basket-wise credit progress, and plan future semesters through an interactive dashboard.

Important: SmartTrack is structured according to the official curriculum advisory from the B.Tech 2025-26 batch only. Students from earlier batches have different credit and basket requirements.

Note: SmartTrack is designed based on IIT Gandhinagar's undergraduate curriculum structure. While the application closely follows the institute advisory, students should always verify important academic decisions using the latest official institute documents.

Humanities and Social Sciences (HSS)

  • Three HSS courses are mandatory.[HS 191: Introduction to Writing I (2 credits), HS 192: Introduction to Writing II (2 credits) and HS 151: Economics (4 credits)]
  • 4 credits must come from General Education (GE) courses.
  • The remaining credits may be completed through HSS or Management electives.

In SmartTrack, GE courses are treated as a sub-category of the HSS basket. Once completed, their credits are automatically counted towards HSS progress.

Mathematics Basket

  • Total requirement: 10 credits
  • Three Mathematics courses are compulsory [MA 103: Calculus of Single Variable and Linear Algebra (4 credits), MA 104: Ordinary Differential Equations (2 credits), MA 203: Numerical Methods (2 credits)]
  • Remaining credits may be earned from any Mathematics basket course.

Science Basket

  • Total requirement: 8 credits
  • Depending on the student's discipline, one Science course may be compulsory.

Basic Science Electives

  • Requirement: 4 credits
  • Every Science basket course is also considered a Basic Science Elective, but not every Basic Science Elective belongs to the Science basket.

Open Electives

  • Requirement: 20 credits
  • These may be selected from any discipline offered by IITGN.

Important: A course contributes to only one basket at a time in general.

Discipline Courses

Every discipline contains:

  • Core Courses (fixed and compulsory)
  • Discipline Electives

Many discipline electives also contain sub-baskets.

For example:

  • Artificial Intelligence contains a CSE sub-basket.
  • Civil Engineering contains multiple elective groups.

Credits earned in a sub-basket are automatically counted towards its parent basket.

3. Getting Started

  1. Create an account.
  2. Log in using your registered credentials.
  3. Start adding your completed courses semester-wise.

4. Dashboard

The Dashboard provides an overview of your academic progress.

Available Information

  • Total completed credits
  • Remaining credits
  • Graduation progress
  • Basket overview
  • Semester SPI trend

Special Requirements

Foundation Programme and General Education courses can be marked separately. Their credits are automatically reflected in the overall graduation progress.

5. Basket Analysis

The Basket Analysis page provides detailed progress for every graduation basket.

For each basket you can view:

  • Required credits
  • Completed credits
  • Remaining credits
  • Progress percentage
  • Courses completed under that basket

Basket Selection

SmartTrack does not automatically decide which basket a course belongs to.

Some IITGN courses may satisfy different baskets depending on a student's curriculum. Therefore, the student is responsible for selecting the appropriate basket while adding a course.

Selecting an incorrect basket may result in inaccurate progress statistics.

6. Semester Planner

The Semester Planner helps students organize future semesters.

Features:

  • Add courses for upcoming semesters.
  • Prevent duplicate course selection.
  • Display projected basket completion.
  • Visualize how future courses contribute toward graduation requirements.

8. Tips for Accurate Tracking

  • Always assign courses to the correct basket.
  • Avoid adding the same course multiple times.
  • Use the Semester Planner only for courses not yet completed.
  • Review Basket Analysis regularly to monitor graduation progress.
  • Remember that Pass/Fail courses do not affect SPI/CPI.

9. Conclusion

SmartTrack brings graduation requirements, basket tracking, semester planning, and academic history into a single platform. By carefully maintaining course records and selecting the appropriate baskets, students can monitor their academic progress throughout their undergraduate program with significantly less manual effort.

Although SmartTrack simplifies academic planning, students should always refer to the latest IIT Gandhinagar advisory for official academic regulations.
